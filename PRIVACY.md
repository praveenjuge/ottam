# Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Ottam is an iPhone-first entertainment application operated by Praveen Juge. This policy explains the information Ottam handles and why.

## Information Ottam handles

- **Account information.** After the first guest episode, listeners may sign in with an email code. Clerk processes authentication and provides Ottam with the account identifier and email address needed to save progress.
- **Story activity.** Ottam stores episode progress, completion state, and first-party session summaries so a listener can resume and review their activity across devices.
- **Movement data.** While a story session is active, the app uses Apple motion and pedometer APIs to classify walking, running, or stationary activity and to show session statistics. Ottam does not request HealthKit access. Raw motion samples are not uploaded.
- **Diagnostics.** Apple platform diagnostics, MetricKit reports, and first-party service logs may be used to maintain reliability and investigate failures. Ottam does not use third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Guest progress and downloaded episodes remain on the device unless the listener signs in and chooses to merge progress. Audio downloads are stored locally for offline playback.

## How information is used and shared

Information is used only to authenticate listeners, deliver and resume stories, synchronize progress, operate the private production system, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Ottam does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Ottam relies on service providers that process information only to provide their contracted functions, including Clerk for authentication, Convex for application data, Cloudflare R2 for private media storage, Vercel for the private administration application, and Apple for app distribution and platform diagnostics.

## Retention, choices, and security

Local guest data can be removed by deleting the app. A signed-in listener may request access to or deletion of account and synchronized activity data by emailing the address below. Operational logs are retained only as reasonably necessary for security and reliability.

Ottam uses access controls, short-lived media URLs, encrypted transport, immutable release records, and least-privilege service credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Children and changes

Ottam is not directed to children under 13. This policy may be updated as the product changes; the effective date above will identify the latest revision.
