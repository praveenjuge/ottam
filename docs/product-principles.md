# Product principles

Ottam delivers stories that can only be experienced by moving. Entertainment, not exercise performance, is the primary user promise.

## Listener experience

- The catalogue feels cinematic and genre-led rather than fitness-led.
- The listener is always the protagonist, but scripts never assume a name, gender, age, appearance, fitness level, or ability.
- Every episode completes in one selected whole-minute duration from 15 through 60 minutes.
- A listener can quit and later resume from the exact playback and plan position.
- The first episode is available to a local guest. Email-code sign-in is offered afterward to preserve progress.

## Story behavior

- Every duration contains the same ordered core plot. Longer plans add authored optional scenes; they never stretch the core with excessive silence.
- Initial plans target 30–40% authored story and 60–70% listener-controlled music.
- Finished scene assets last 20–90 seconds so external audio can duck briefly and predictably.
- Reactive moments select walking or running flavor at authored checkpoints, then converge on the same plot.
- Stationary movement pauses story progression. Walking slowly or declining to run never causes failure, punishment, lost content, or a negative score.

## Production behavior

- Production audio is generated and mixed ahead of release, never during a listener session.
- Each release is immutable and includes metadata, artwork, compiled plans, and all core, optional, walking, and running audio needed offline.
- The chat-driven studio proposes revision-bound changes. A human explicitly approves every content or audio side effect and is the only actor allowed to publish.
- Production audio generation remains disabled during implementation, tests, fixtures, seeding, previews, and deployment.

## Explicit exclusions

The initial product has no HealthKit, Apple Watch, subscriptions, social features, leaderboards, achievements, location-aware stories, routes, maps, live personalized generation, Android client, or public website.
